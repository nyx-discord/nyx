nyx

A Discord bot framework that stays out of your way.

Let nyx handle commands, events and schedules, leaving your process, build step and architecture to you. Use discord.js or @discordjs/core, through the gateway or over HTTP.

Whether you want a single file, no persistence, commands created in runtime, or anything else, nyx doesn't interfere, and you can replace any part of it.

npm i @nyx-discord/djs discord.js
